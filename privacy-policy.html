Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how applications and games published by Palash Parate may collect, use, and protect information when you use our applications.

Unless specifically stated within an application, we do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, postal address, phone number, or email address.

This information may include device information, application version, operating system version, crash information, performance data, and other technical information required to operate and improve the application.

How Information May Be Used

Information processed by our applications may be used to:

  • Improve gameplay and user experience.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the application.

Some applications may use third-party services for features such as analytics, crash reporting, advertisements, authentication, cloud services, multiplayer functionality, or Google Play services.

These third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The third-party services used may vary depending on the specific application.

Device Permissions

Some applications may request access to certain device features or permissions when those permissions are necessary for a particular feature.

Permissions are only intended to be used for the functionality described within the application.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers when required to provide application functionality, maintain security, provide technical services, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information processed through our applications.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Any information processed by an application or its third-party services may be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the relevant functionality, comply with legal requirements, or fulfill legitimate operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children unless explicitly stated for a specific application and handled in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements.

If you believe that personal information from a child has been provided through one of our applications, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.
